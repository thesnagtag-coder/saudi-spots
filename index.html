Camping rules

• Camping in national parks, natural pastures and forests needs a permit from the NCVC "Nabati" platform. Camping there without one can be fined. • One camping permit per person per season. • Leave the site clean, drive only on marked tracks, and don't burn local wood or charcoal. • Before any desert trip: tell someone your route and return time, go with 2 cars if possible, carry extra water, and download offline maps. • How busy each campsite is has not been checked - add your own notes after each visit.
Source: Saudi Press Agency

Using it in the car

Tap Google, Waze or Apple (iPhone only) on any spot. The route opens in that maps app on your phone. If your car has Apple CarPlay or Android Auto, that maps app shows on the car screen as usual.
Waze and Apple buttons need exact GPS. Spots with only a link or plus code open in Google Maps.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari): Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Android (Chrome): menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
